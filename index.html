One consistent visual language

A boxed × marks an irreversible step, a star marks a rate-limiting step, and every new pathway added to the map follows the same colour and symbol rules.

This is an unofficial student project. It is not produced, reviewed or endorsed by the New York College of Podiatric Medicine. Always check your own lecture material before an exam.

Carbohydrate metabolism map

Gluconeogenesis runs up the left, glycolysis down the right, meeting at glucose and pyruvate; one oxaloacetate branch point feeds both the Krebs cycle and gluconeogenesis. Below that: the electron transport chain, the glycerol 3-phosphate shuttle, glycogen metabolism, fructose / galactose / polyol, the pentose phosphate pathway, and GAGs and glycoproteins — all joined to the main map by dashed linking arrows. Quantities are per glucose (×2 below the aldolase split).
